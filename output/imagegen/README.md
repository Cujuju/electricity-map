## Midnight Grid variations

- [Full-bleed square, navy background](round-3-icons/02-midnight-grid-full-bleed.png) · [Edit prompt](round-3-icons/02-midnight-grid-full-bleed-prompt.txt)
- [Full-bleed square, purple background from Contour Light](round-3-icons/02-midnight-grid-purple-full-bleed.png) · [Edit prompt](round-3-icons/02-midnight-grid-purple-full-bleed-prompt.txt)

The full-bleed variations remove the artificial rounded-square frame. Both retain the blue U.S. map, cyan electrical paths and three gold nodes. The electrical paths and terrain are artistic branding elements, not geographic data.

The individual PNGs and prompts are the versioned archive. Download ZIPs are convenience copies and are not required to revisit the concepts.
